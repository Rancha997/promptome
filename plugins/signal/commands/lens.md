# /lens

Review and update your opportunity lens.

Use this when your strategic focus has changed and you want a full
structured review of your lens. Claude will interview you about
what changed and rewrite ~/Documents/Signal/lens.md and ~/Documents/Signal/sources.md.

For small adjustments, just tell Claude directly in chat —
e.g. "focus less on n8n and more on Cowork plugins" — and Claude
will update the lens file inline without a full review.

Usage: /lens
